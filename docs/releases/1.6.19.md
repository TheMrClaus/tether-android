What's new
- The browser pane shows what the agent is doing: a marker where it acts, and a note when you take over.
- Switch between the browser's tabs from the pane, and pick a named browser profile for each session.
- Settings has a Browser profiles section to create, rename and delete profiles that keep your logins.
- The browser pane has a cleaner look, with clearer loading, empty and error states.
- On wide windows the browser pane sits beside the chat instead of covering it.
